# Learning-First Skills

Skills for agentic coding that keep the developer cognitively engaged in design, so they remain the holder of their system's theory.

## Language

**Theory**:
The developer's working understanding of a specific system: why it is shaped the way it is and how it would respond to change (in Naur's sense). The primary thing these skills protect and build.
_Avoid_: Mental model, design model, understanding

**Skill formation**:
The developer's general, cross-project growth in design ability and vocabulary. A deliberate secondary outcome, reached through reveals that name the design concept involved.
_Avoid_: Learning (when used to mean this specifically)

**Area**:
A part of the system the developer holds Theory about, named in the developer's own terms.
_Avoid_: Subsystem, component, module (when meaning this)

**Ripple question**:
A grill question asked right after a Lock-in, in which the developer predicts what existing code depends on what the decision changes; checked in the session against the current code.
_Avoid_: Change-impact drill, checkpoint, impact analysis

**Commitment**:
The developer's own answer to a question, given before Claude's view is shown.
_Avoid_: Guess, first answer

**Lock-in**:
The developer's final, informed choice on a question, made after the critique and Claude's recommendation. May keep or revise the Commitment.
_Avoid_: Confirmation, approval

**Triage rubric**:
The short rule that sets a question's depth from its stakes and its novelty to the developer. Never decides whether a skill runs.
_Avoid_: Triage (alone; collides with issue-tracker triage)

**Stakes**:
How much a decision matters: the higher of its reversibility and its blast radius.
_Avoid_: Risk, importance

**Depth**:
How much of an exercise the developer is asked to do: full, light, or offered a skip.
_Avoid_: Intensity, level

**Decision type**:
The kind of design call a decision is (e.g. where state lives, error handling, API shape). A tag on a decision, never an Area.
_Avoid_: Category, decision area
